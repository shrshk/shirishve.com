# Credits

Every drawing on this site is original and is drawn by the code in `src/lib/doodle.ts` and
`src/components/doodle/` when the site is built. There are no images. The paper texture is an SVG
filter. The look follows my own doodle games (https://games.shirishve.com).

## Fonts

Self-hosted from `public/fonts`, Latin subset, all under the SIL Open Font License 1.1.

| Font         | Used for       | Source                                      |
| ------------ | -------------- | ------------------------------------------- |
| Caveat       | Titles, labels | https://github.com/googlefonts/caveat       |
| Patrick Hand | Body text      | https://github.com/googlefonts/patrick-hand |

Caveat is from its Fontsource package. Patrick Hand is the Latin subset from Google Fonts.

## Icons

Brand icons are from Simple Icons (https://simpleicons.org, CC0 1.0), unmodified. Each brand's
trademark belongs to its owner. A platform that Simple Icons does not carry gets plain letters.

## Club

The club is not named. It shows as a red thread and a red and white scarf, both original drawings. No crest, wordmark, kit or
sponsor mark is used. This site has no connection to the club.
